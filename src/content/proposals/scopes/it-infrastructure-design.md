## SCOPE OF SERVICES

**IT Infrastructure Consulting and Design**

### 1. Project Overview

This Scope of Services outlines the deliverables, responsibilities, and approach for providing comprehensive IT infrastructure consulting and design services. The objective is to assess current infrastructure, develop a strategic technology roadmap, and deliver detailed design specifications that align with business objectives and industry best practices.

### 2. Scope of Work

#### 2.1 Discovery and Assessment Phase

The consultant will conduct a comprehensive analysis of the existing IT infrastructure, including:

- Current state infrastructure assessment including servers, storage, networking equipment, virtualization platforms, and cloud services
- Network topology documentation and performance analysis
- Security posture evaluation including firewalls, access controls, encryption, and compliance requirements
- Disaster recovery and business continuity capability review
- Application inventory and dependencies mapping
- IT room and data center space utilization assessment
- Wireless network performance and coverage analysis
- Existing cabling infrastructure evaluation and documentation
- Stakeholder interviews with IT staff, department heads, and key business users
- Documentation review of existing policies, procedures, and architectural diagrams

#### 2.2 Gap Analysis and Requirements Definition

Based on the discovery findings, the consultant will:

- Identify gaps between current state and desired future state
- Define technical and business requirements for infrastructure improvements
- Document performance, scalability, and reliability requirements
- Assess compliance and regulatory requirements applicable to the organization
- Prioritize infrastructure needs based on business impact and technical dependencies

#### 2.3 Infrastructure Design and Architecture

The consultant will develop detailed infrastructure designs covering:

- Network architecture including LAN, WAN, wireless, and remote access solutions
- Server and storage architecture with virtualization and cloud integration strategies
- IT room and data center layout design with equipment rack elevations
- Power distribution and cooling requirements for IT spaces
- Wireless access point placement and coverage mapping with heat maps
- Structured cabling infrastructure design including horizontal and vertical pathways
- Fiber optic backbone design and specifications
- Cable management and labeling standards
- Security architecture including network segmentation, access controls, and monitoring systems
- Disaster recovery and backup architecture
- Cloud strategy and hybrid infrastructure design (if applicable)
- High availability and redundancy design for critical systems

#### 2.4 Technology Recommendations and Vendor Selection

The consultant will provide:

- Evaluation of technology platforms and solutions aligned with business requirements
- Vendor comparison and selection criteria
- Hardware and software specifications and sizing recommendations
- Wireless access point and controller specifications
- Structured cabling components and materials specifications
- Rack and cabinet specifications with PDU requirements
- Total cost of ownership (TCO) analysis for proposed solutions
- Licensing and procurement guidance

#### 2.5 Implementation Roadmap and Migration Strategy

The consultant will develop:

- Phased implementation plan with timelines and milestones
- Migration strategy for transitioning from current to future state
- Risk assessment and mitigation strategies for the implementation
- Resource requirements including internal staff and external partners
- Change management recommendations for minimizing business disruption

#### 2.6 Documentation and Knowledge Transfer

Comprehensive documentation deliverables will include:

- Current state assessment report with findings and recommendations
- Detailed design documentation including network diagrams, architecture diagrams, and technical specifications
- IT room layout drawings with equipment placement and dimensions
- Wireless coverage heat maps and access point placement diagrams
- Structured cabling plans including pathway drawings and port schedules
- Bill of materials (BOM) for hardware and software requirements
- Implementation project plan and timeline
- Cost estimates and budget recommendations
- Knowledge transfer sessions with IT team to review designs and recommendations

### 3. Project Deliverables

The following deliverables will be provided upon completion of the engagement:

1. Infrastructure Assessment Report
1. Gap Analysis and Requirements Document
1. Target Architecture Design Documentation
1. Network Topology Diagrams (current and future state)
1. IT Room Layout and Equipment Rack Elevation Drawings
1. Wireless Site Survey Report and Coverage Heat Maps
1. Structured Cabling Design Plans and Specifications
1. Technical Specifications and Configuration Standards
1. Technology Vendor Recommendations and Comparison Matrix
1. Bill of Materials with Detailed Pricing
1. Implementation Roadmap and Project Plan
1. Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan
1. Executive Summary Presentation

### 4. Out of Scope

The following items are specifically excluded from this scope of services:

- Physical implementation or installation of infrastructure components
- Hardware or software procurement and purchasing
- Physical cabling installation and termination
- Application development or custom software solutions
- Ongoing managed services or technical support
- Training programs for end users or IT staff
- Project management for infrastructure implementation (unless explicitly added to scope)

### 5. Assumptions and Dependencies

This scope of services is based on the following assumptions:

- Client will provide timely access to existing infrastructure, systems, and documentation
- Physical access to IT rooms, data centers, and all areas requiring wireless coverage will be provided
- Building floor plans and architectural drawings will be made available for planning purposes
- Key stakeholders and technical staff will be available for interviews and review sessions
- Client will provide a dedicated point of contact for coordination and decision-making
- Remote access to systems will be provided where necessary for assessment activities
- Existing documentation, network diagrams, and asset inventories will be made available
- Client will provide timely feedback and approvals at project milestones

### 6. Estimated Timeline

The consulting engagement is estimated to be completed within 8-12 weeks from project initiation, subject to client availability and complexity of the existing environment. A detailed project schedule will be developed during the kickoff phase.

| Phase | Duration |
|---|---|
| Discovery and Assessment | 2-3 weeks |
| Gap Analysis and Requirements | 1-2 weeks |
| Design and Architecture | 3-4 weeks |
| Technology Recommendations | 1-2 weeks |
| Implementation Roadmap | 1 week |
| Documentation and Knowledge Transfer | 1 week |

### 7. Client Responsibilities

To ensure project success, the client agrees to:

- Designate a project sponsor and primary point of contact
- Provide access to facilities, systems, and personnel as required
- Make key stakeholders available for scheduled meetings and interviews
- Provide existing documentation, diagrams, and relevant information in a timely manner
- Review and approve deliverables within agreed-upon timeframes
- Communicate any changes in requirements or priorities promptly

### 8. Acceptance Criteria

Each deliverable will be considered complete upon client review and written acceptance. The client will have five business days to review each deliverable and provide feedback. If no feedback is provided within this timeframe, the deliverable will be deemed accepted.

### 9. Change Management Process

Any changes to this scope of services must be documented through a formal change request process. Changes that materially affect project timeline, budget, or deliverables will require written approval from both parties before implementation.

### 10. Additional Considerations

**Confidentiality:** All information shared during this engagement will be treated as confidential and used solely for the purpose of delivering the consulting services outlined in this document.

**Intellectual Property:** All deliverables and work products created specifically for this project will become the property of the client upon final payment. The consultant retains the right to use generalized methodologies and approaches.

**Support Post-Delivery:** The consultant will provide reasonable clarification and support for delivered documentation for 30 days following project completion at no additional charge. Extended support or implementation assistance can be arranged under a separate agreement.
