**Hospitality Technology Implementation Services**

[Property Name] | [Date]

**Scope includes:**

- Meet with the client team to discern their hospitality technology requirements across all food & beverage, guest-facing, and back-of-house operations.
- Conduct technology visioning workshops to define the guest experience, operational workflows, and system integration goals.
- Attend focused design meetings with Architect, Interior Designer, MEP, and other project team members.
  - Lead or participate in page turn and design review meetings.
  - Review brand standards and management company technology requirements for compliance.

**Point of Sale (POS) Systems**

- System evaluation and selection advisory for all F&B outlets, banquet, retail, and ancillary revenue centers.
  - POS terminal hardware layout and placement design per outlet.
  - Kitchen Display System (KDS) workflow and station routing design.
  - Menu hierarchy, modifier, and pricing tier configuration planning.
  - Payment gateway integration and PCI-DSS compliance coordination.
  - POS-to-PMS posting and charge routing interface design.
  - Network and infrastructure requirements for POS including VLAN segmentation and failover.

**Property Management System (PMS)**

- PMS platform evaluation, selection advisory, and deployment model recommendation (cloud, on-premise, hybrid).
  - Room type, rate structure, and reservation routing configuration planning.
  - Interface and integration design for all PMS-connected systems:
    - POS, CRS, RMS, door locks, telephony, IPTV, energy management, guest messaging, accounting, and CRM.
  - Reporting and analytics requirements including night audit, financial, housekeeping, and management KPIs.
  - User role and permission architecture design.
  - Data migration planning for property conversions or platform changes.

**Central Reservation System (CRS) & Channel Management**

- CRS platform evaluation and selection based on distribution strategy and brand affiliation.
  - Booking engine design for direct web and mobile reservations.
  - Channel manager integration for OTA, GDS, and meta-search connectivity.
  - Two-way interface design between CRS, PMS, and Revenue Management System.
  - Rate parity, inventory allocation, and overbooking parameter configuration planning.
  - Loyalty and membership program integration requirements.

**In-Room Entertainment, IPTV & Guest Room Experience**

- In-room technology experience design including content delivery, interactive services, and room controls.
  - IPTV and content distribution system architecture (headend, middleware, IP multicast).
  - Guest room control system specification (lighting, HVAC, shades, DND/MUR indicators).
  - Casting and BYOD connectivity design (Chromecast, AirPlay) with network isolation.
  - In-room display specification, sizing, and mounting coordination with millwork.
  - PMS integration for guest welcome, preferences, folio display, and express checkout.
  - Network infrastructure requirements per guest room (VLAN, bandwidth, switch density).
  - Pilot room design and property-wide rollout planning.

**Guest Wi-Fi & Network Infrastructure**

- Wireless network design including AP placement, coverage mapping, channel planning, and density calculations.
  - Guest Wi-Fi gateway and captive portal design (tiered plans, PMS authentication, branding).
  - Network architecture design (core/distribution/access, VLAN segmentation, QoS).
  - Internet bandwidth sizing and ISP circuit recommendations.
  - Guest network isolation and security design.
  - Back-of-house network design for operational systems.
  - Structured cabling infrastructure design and specification.
  - Provide Predictive Wireless Access Point Study.

**Digital Key & Mobile Guest Experience**

- Digital key platform evaluation based on lock hardware compatibility and PMS integration.
  - Mobile check-in/checkout workflow design including identity verification and room assignment logic.
  - Door lock readiness assessment (BLE/NFC capability, firmware, encoder compatibility).
  - Elevator and common area access control integration for digital key credentials.
  - Guest journey mapping from pre-arrival through post-departure.

**Telephony & Voice Communications**

- Telephony platform evaluation (on-premise PBX, cloud, hybrid) and selection advisory.
  - Guest room telephone specification and provisioning design.
  - PMS-to-PBX interface design (room status, guest name, call billing, voicemail, wake-up).
  - Administrative communications design (front desk consoles, hunt groups, paging).
  - SIP trunking, carrier circuit design, and E911 compliance.

**Digital Signage**

- Digital signage needs assessment including display locations, content types, and audience.
  - Display hardware specification (size, resolution, brightness, orientation, media player).
  - CMS evaluation and selection (cloud vs. on-premise, multi-property management).
  - Content zone and template design for lobby, meeting rooms, restaurants, wayfinding, and elevators.
  - Dynamic content feed integration from PMS, event management, and POS systems.

**Energy Management Systems**

- EMS evaluation and selection based on HVAC type, building construction, and sustainability goals.
  - Occupancy-based guest room energy management design.
  - PMS and door lock integration for check-in/checkout triggered temperature preconditioning.
  - Dashboard and reporting requirements for energy monitoring and savings tracking.
  - Coordination with MEP engineers and BMS integrators.

**Housekeeping & Engineering Operations**

- Operations platform evaluation and selection for task management, service delivery, and maintenance tracking.
  - Workflow and task automation design (room assignments, preventive maintenance, escalation rules).
  - PMS integration for real-time room status, guest preferences, and VIP alerts.
  - Mobile device strategy and specification for housekeeping and engineering staff.
  - Integration with guest messaging for service request fulfillment.

**Spa, Recreation & Amenity Management**

- Spa and recreation management platform evaluation and selection.
  - Appointment booking and resource scheduling configuration design.
  - PMS and POS integration for charge posting, loyalty accrual, and guest profiles.
  - Online and mobile booking engine design for spa, fitness, and recreational activities.

**Payment Processing & Gateway Integration**

- Payment gateway evaluation and selection across all revenue systems (PMS, POS, spa, online booking).
  - Payment integration architecture with tokenization and end-to-end encryption.
  - PCI-DSS scope assessment and network segmentation advisory.
  - EMV, contactless (NFC), and mobile wallet support requirements.
  - Pre-authorization, incremental auth, and settlement workflow design.

**Physical Security & Access Control**

- Security technology needs assessment including threat analysis and coverage requirements.
  - Video surveillance system design:
    - Camera placement, field of view calculations, resolution requirements, storage retention, and analytics.
  - Access control system design:
    - Credential types, reader placement, door hardware, and controller architecture.
  - Video management system (VMS) specification including server sizing and storage calculations.
  - Integration between access control, PMS, and digital key systems.
  - Network infrastructure requirements (dedicated VLANs, bandwidth, PoE switch sizing).
  - Emergency and life safety system integration advisory.

**Revenue Management Systems**

- RMS platform evaluation and selection based on property type and pricing strategy.
  - PMS and CRS integration design for real-time data exchange.
  - Configuration advisory for forecasting models, pricing rules, and competitive rate shopping.
  - Reporting and dashboard requirements for revenue analytics.

**Guest Messaging, CRM & Communication Platforms**

- Guest messaging platform evaluation (SMS, WhatsApp, in-app, web chat).
  - CRM platform evaluation for guest profile management and personalized marketing.
  - PMS integration for guest data sync, stay history, and automated communications.
  - Guest journey communication workflow design (pre-arrival through post-departure).
  - AI chatbot and virtual concierge evaluation.
  - Data privacy and consent management advisory (GDPR, CCPA).

**Procurement & Bid Management**

- Provide an Issue for Bid Bill of Materials for approval that includes a Request for Proposal and Bill of Materials for accurate scope alignment across all technology disciplines.
  - Review and level technology bids across all system categories.
  - Vendor contract review and negotiation support.

**Coordination & Responsibility Matrix**

- Provide a draft responsibility matrix (RACI) to delineate responsibilities between Owner, Consultant, General Contractor, Sub-Contractors, Technology Vendors, and Hotel Operator.
  - Develop and maintain the master technology schedule aligned with the construction schedule.
  - Conduct regular technology coordination meetings and produce meeting minutes and action item logs.
  - Review and approve technology vendor submittals, shop drawings, and RFI responses.
  - Perform site visits for installation quality assurance and progress verification.
