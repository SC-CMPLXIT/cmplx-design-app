**Scope includes:**

- Meet with the client team to discern their technical requirements.
- Attend focused design meetings with Architect, MEP and other project team members
  - Lead or participate in page turn & design meetings
- Design Drawings for IT Infrastructure, Audio Visual, & Security through Construction Drawings including:
  - IT Infrastructure Specific:
    - IT Room Layout – rack placement and coordination of services
    - Carrier Infrastructure – ISP Services into the property
    - Fiber and Copper Plant
    - Provide Predictive Wireless Access Point Study
    - Rack Elevations & Termination Schedule
    - Infrastructure Requirements
      - Conduit, Electrical Boxes, & Pathways
  - Audio Visual Specific:
    - Control System Specification & Location
    - Define requirements for additional system inputs
    - Speakers Models & Locations
    - System Cabling requirements
    - Infrastructure Requirements
      - Conduit, Electrical Boxes, & Pathway
    - AV equipment requirements in the IT Room, including rack placement, if necessary.
    - Power requirements at AV locations and in IT Room
  - IP Based Security Specific:
    - Camera Placement, Door Elevations, Equipment Specifications
    - IT Room Requirements and Elevations
    - System Cabling requirements
    - Infrastructure Requirements
      - Conduit, Electrical Boxes, & Pathways
- Provide an Issue for Bill of Materials for approval that includes a Request for Proposal and Bill of Materials for accurate scope alignment.
- Review and level Technology Bids
- Provide a draft responsibility matrix to delineate responsibilities between Owner, Consultant, General Contractor, Sub-Contractors, and Integrator.
