## SCOPE OF SERVICES

**Technical Program Management**

### 1. Overview

This Scope of Services outlines the technical program management services to be provided. The Technical Program Manager (TPM) will be responsible for planning, executing, and delivering complex technical programs and initiatives across multiple teams and stakeholders.

### 2. Core Responsibilities

#### 2.1 Program Planning and Strategy

- Define program scope, objectives, and success criteria in alignment with business goals
- Develop comprehensive program roadmaps and execution plans
- Identify dependencies, critical paths, and potential risks across multiple workstreams
- Establish program governance structures, decision-making frameworks, and escalation paths

#### 2.2 Execution and Delivery

- Coordinate cross-functional teams including engineering, product, design, and operations
- Drive milestone achievement and ensure timely delivery of program deliverables
- Manage program schedules, resources, and budget allocation
- Facilitate technical decision-making and resolve blockers across teams

#### 2.3 Stakeholder Management and Communication

- Provide regular program status updates to executives and key stakeholders
- Facilitate program reviews, retrospectives, and steering committee meetings
- Manage stakeholder expectations and ensure alignment across the organization
- Create and maintain program documentation, dashboards, and reporting mechanisms

#### 2.4 Risk and Issue Management

- Identify, assess, and mitigate technical and operational risks
- Develop contingency plans and alternative approaches for critical program elements
- Track and resolve program issues, escalating when necessary
- Maintain risk registers and issue logs with clear ownership and resolution timelines

### 3. Key Deliverables

| Deliverable | Description |
|---|---|
| Program Charter | Comprehensive document defining program objectives, scope, success metrics, and governance |
| Program Roadmap | Timeline with milestones, dependencies, and deliverable schedules |
| Status Reports | Weekly/bi-weekly program status updates including progress, risks, and blockers |
| Risk Register | Living document tracking identified risks, mitigation strategies, and ownership |
| Post-Implementation Review | Assessment of outcomes, lessons learned, and recommendations for future programs |

### 4. Success Metrics

- On-time delivery of program milestones and deliverables
- Achievement of defined program objectives and key results
- Stakeholder satisfaction scores and feedback
- Effective risk mitigation with minimal critical issues
- Quality of deliverables as measured against acceptance criteria

### 5. Communication and Reporting

#### 5.1 Regular Cadence

- Weekly status reports to program stakeholders
- Bi-weekly steering committee meetings with executive leadership
- Monthly program review presentations

#### 5.2 Ad-Hoc Communication

- Immediate escalation of critical issues or risks
- On-demand updates for urgent stakeholder inquiries

### 6. Duration and Scope Boundaries

This scope of services covers technical program management activities for the duration of the program lifecycle, from initiation through closure. Any significant changes to program scope, objectives, or resource requirements will be documented and approved through the change management process.

#### 6.1 In Scope

- Technical program planning, execution, and delivery management
- Cross-functional coordination and stakeholder management
- Risk management, issue resolution, and program governance

#### 6.2 Out of Scope

- Direct people management or performance reviews
- Technical implementation or hands-on development work
- Product management or product strategy definition
- Budget approval authority (TPM provides recommendations only)

### 7. Assumptions and Dependencies

- Access to necessary tools, systems, and program documentation
- Timely availability of subject matter experts and key stakeholders
- Appropriate decision-making authority delegated to the TPM
- Support from executive sponsors for escalations and strategic decisions
- Cooperation and engagement from cross-functional team members
