## 1. Introduction

CMPLXIT provides independent technology consulting services for hospitality properties including hotels, private membership clubs, branded residences, and mixed-use developments. Our services span the full project lifecycle from pre-design technology planning through post-opening stabilization, covering system evaluation, design, specification, procurement advisory, implementation oversight, integration coordination, and operational readiness.

This document defines the scope of consulting services available across all hospitality technology disciplines. Not all service areas will apply to every engagement. The specific services, deliverables, and fee structure for each project will be defined in a project-specific proposal that references applicable sections of this document.

Items marked with brackets [ ] are project-specific variables to be confirmed at the time of proposal issuance.

## 2. Engagement Phases

CMPLXIT consulting engagements are structured across the following phases. Each phase may be engaged independently or as part of a comprehensive technology program management engagement.

| Phase 1: Pre-Design & Planning | Technology visioning workshops, brand standards review, existing systems assessment, and technology master planning. Deliverables include a Technology Design Narrative and preliminary budget estimate. |
|---|---|
| Phase 2: Design & Specification | Detailed system design, equipment specification, integration mapping, infrastructure coordination with MEP/architect, and production of technology bid documents. Deliverables include equipment schedules, riser diagrams, room detail drawings, and integration matrices. |
| Phase 3: Procurement Advisory | Vendor evaluation, bid analysis, contract review, and equipment procurement support. CMPLXIT acts as the owner’s representative to ensure competitive pricing and specification compliance. |
| Phase 4: Implementation Oversight | On-site and remote coordination of vendor installation activities, schedule management, progress tracking, issue resolution, and quality assurance inspections. |
| Phase 5: Integration & Commissioning | System integration testing, interface validation, punch list management, and user acceptance testing. Deliverables include commissioning reports and system performance verification documentation. |
| Phase 6: Operational Readiness & Turnover | Staff technology training coordination, operational documentation review, as-built verification, warranty registration, and post-opening stabilization support. |

## 4. General Consulting Services

In addition to the technology-specific service areas defined in Section 3, CMPLXIT provides the following cross-disciplinary consulting services that apply across all technology categories.

### 4.1  Technology Program Management

1. Serve as the owner’s technology representative across all project phases, coordinating with architects, interior designers, MEP engineers, general contractors, and technology vendors.
1. Develop and maintain the master technology schedule, aligning milestones with the overall construction and FF&E schedule.
1. Manage the technology budget, tracking committed costs, change orders, and forecasted expenditures against the approved budget.
1. Conduct regular technology coordination meetings and produce meeting minutes, action item logs, and progress reports.
1. Review and approve technology vendor submittals, shop drawings, and RFI responses.
1. Perform site visits for installation quality assurance, progress verification, and issue resolution.

### 4.2  Infrastructure Coordination

1. Coordinate technology infrastructure requirements (power, data, conduit, pathways, cooling) with the MEP engineer, architect, and general contractor.
1. Produce or review technology room layouts for MDF, IDF, server rooms, and head-end equipment spaces including rack elevations, power, cooling, and access requirements.
1. Review architectural and MEP drawings for technology impacts and issue comments during each design phase (SD, DD, CD).
1. Coordinate structured cabling design and pathway requirements between the low-voltage contractor, architect, and technology vendors.

### 4.3  Vendor Management

1. Prepare technology bid packages and manage the competitive bid process for all technology systems.
1. Evaluate vendor proposals for specification compliance, pricing competitiveness, and operational capability.
1. Assist with vendor contract negotiation, including scope, schedule, payment terms, warranties, and service level agreements.
1. Monitor vendor performance throughout installation and commissioning, escalating issues as needed.

### 4.4  Standards and Documentation

1. Develop property-specific technology design standards for use across multiple venues, floors, or properties within a portfolio.
1. Produce technology design narratives, equipment specifications, riser diagrams, integration matrices, and room detail drawings.
1. Review and verify vendor-produced as-built documentation, system manuals, and warranty documentation for completeness and accuracy.
1. Create operational technology guides and quick-reference materials for property engineering and IT staff.

## 5. Standard Deliverables

The following deliverables are representative of CMPLXIT’s work product. Project-specific deliverables will be defined in the project proposal.

| Deliverable | Description |
|---|---|
| Technology Design Narrative | Comprehensive document describing the technology vision, system descriptions, integration requirements, and design criteria for all technology disciplines on the project. |
| Equipment Schedules | Itemized lists of all specified equipment by system, including manufacturer, model, quantity, unit cost, and extended cost. |
| Riser Diagrams | System-level diagrams showing signal flow, connectivity, and infrastructure pathways between equipment locations. |
| Integration Matrix | Cross-reference document mapping all system-to-system interfaces, including protocol, data exchange, responsible party, and testing requirements. |
| Room Detail Drawings | Location-specific drawings showing equipment placement, cable routing, outlet locations, and coordination details for each technology-equipped space. |
| Bid Documents | Complete bid packages including scope of work, equipment schedules, drawings, and evaluation criteria for competitive procurement. |
| Commissioning Reports | Documented results of system testing, punch list items, and acceptance criteria verification for each technology system. |
| As-Built Verification | Review and markup of vendor-produced as-built drawings to confirm accuracy against installed conditions. |
